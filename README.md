Problem Statement

The online education industry has experienced rapid growth, leading to intense competition among EdTech platforms offering recorded courses across various domains. For a new EdTech startup, identifying profitable course categories, understanding learner preferences, evaluating instructor performance, and analyzing engagement metrics are critical for making informed business decisions.

The challenge lies in transforming large volumes of course data containing information such as categories, ratings, languages, instructors, viewership, course duration, subtitles, and learner engagement into meaningful insights. This project aims to leverage Power BI and data analytics techniques to analyze the competitive landscape of online courses and provide actionable recommendations that can help the startup optimize its course offerings, improve learner engagement, and develop effective market-entry strategies.

Final Findings
High-Demand Categories Identified
Analysis revealed that certain course categories consistently attract higher viewership and learner engagement, indicating strong market demand and revenue potential.
Course Ratings Influence Popularity
Courses with higher ratings generally received greater viewership and engagement, highlighting the importance of maintaining content quality and learner satisfaction.
Multi-Language Accessibility Improves Reach
Courses offering subtitles in multiple languages demonstrated broader audience reach, suggesting that localization can significantly expand market penetration.
Instructor Performance Impacts Course Success
Top-performing instructors consistently achieved higher ratings and engagement levels, making instructor quality a key factor in course success.
Standardized Duration Enables Better Comparison
Converting different duration formats into a common "Total Hours" metric allowed accurate comparison of course length and its relationship with learner engagement.
Engagement Rate Provides Deeper Insights
The engagement rate (Likes ÷ Views) proved to be a more reliable performance indicator than viewership alone, helping identify courses that genuinely resonate with learners.
Skill Demand Trends Were Identified
Analysis of course skills highlighted the most frequently taught and sought-after competencies, providing guidance for future course development.
Data-Driven Business Recommendations
The dashboard enables stakeholders to identify profitable categories, select high-performing instructors, prioritize multilingual content, and focus on highly engaging courses for strategic growth.
Conclusion

The project successfully transformed raw online course data into actionable business intelligence using Power BI. The insights generated can help an EdTech startup make informed decisions regarding course portfolio planning, instructor selection, content localization, and learner engagement strategies, ultimately improving competitiveness in the online education market.
